01 · Two ways to pay

Pay with ETH, or with USDT.

A Recommended · Easiest

Pay with ETH

One transaction and you're done. Uniswap converts your ETH through the SECT/USDT pool automatically, and the network fee comes out of the same ETH.

  • One transaction: the swap
  • No approval needed
  • You only need ETH in your wallet
Buy with ETH↗
B Also possible

Pay with USDT

Swap USDT for #SECT directly in the pool. The first time, Uniswap asks you to approve USDT: that is one extra transaction, and both are paid in ETH.

  • USDT on Ethereum (ERC-20)
  • Approve once, then swap
  • Keep some ETH for the network fee
Buy with USDT↗

Always keep some ETH in your wallet.

Every transaction on Ethereum — the approval and the swap — pays its network fee in ETH, even when you buy with USDT. Without ETH, your wallet can't send anything. Keep enough for a few transactions; your wallet shows the exact fee before you confirm.

Selling

Sell whenever you want.

Selling is the same swap in reverse: choose #SECT in “Sell” and ETH or USDT in “Buy”. The pool is open 24 hours a day and there is no token tax on sells.

03 · Step by step

From zero to #SECT in nine steps.

  1. 01/ 09

    Wallet

    Install MetaMask

    Download it only from metamask.io or your phone's official app store. Create a new wallet and write the Secret Recovery Phrase on paper. Never type it into a website and never share it.

    TipOn a phone use the MetaMask app; on a computer, the browser extension. No one from Sectora will ever ask for your phrase.
    metamask.io ↗
  2. 02/ 09

    Funds

    Add ETH (or USDT) to your wallet

    Buy ETH on an exchange, or with a card inside MetaMask, and send it to your wallet address. When you withdraw from an exchange, choose the Ethereum (ERC-20) network.

    Paying with USDT?Send USDT on Ethereum — USDT on Tron, BNB Chain or other networks won't reach this pool — and send a little ETH too, for the fees.
  3. 03/ 09

    Uniswap

    Open Uniswap

    Go to app.uniswap.org — check the address — or use the buttons on this page: they open Uniswap with #SECT already selected.

    Open Uniswap with #SECT ↗
    On a phoneOpen the link from the browser built into the MetaMask app (or your wallet's app). That way the wallet connects without leaving the page.
  4. 04/ 09

    Connect

    Connect your wallet

    Press Connect, choose MetaMask (or your wallet) and approve the connection in the wallet. Check that the network is Ethereum.

    Good to knowConnecting is free and does not move any funds. It only lets Uniswap see your address and balances.
  5. 05/ 09

    Tokens

    Choose what you pay and #SECT

    In Sell, choose ETH or USDT. In Buy, choose #SECT; if it doesn't appear, paste the contract 0x8C9984B06281f1CA9416e493c2E602AaB08513db.

    A warning may appearUniswap may warn that the token isn't on its default list. That's normal for tokens outside its lists: check that the address matches exactly, then continue.
  6. 06/ 09

    Amount

    Enter the amount and review

    Type how much you want to spend. Before going on, read the rate, the price impact, the minimum received and the network fee.

    If it failsIf the swap fails because the price moved, raise the slippage slightly in the settings (for example to 1–3%) and try again.
  7. 07/ 09

    Approval Only with USDT

    Approve USDT (first time only)

    The first time you pay with USDT, Uniswap asks for permission to use it. Confirm it in your wallet: it's a transaction with a network fee in ETH. Uniswap may also ask you to sign a message, which is free.

    Paying with ETH?Skip this step: ETH needs no approval.
  8. 08/ 09

    Swap

    Swap and confirm

    Press Swap, review the summary and confirm in your wallet. Ethereum usually confirms in under a minute; Uniswap tells you when it's done.

    Check the feeYour wallet shows the network fee before you confirm. If you don't have enough ETH for it, the wallet won't let you continue.
  9. 09/ 09

    Done

    See #SECT in your wallet

    If MetaMask doesn't show #SECT yet, choose Import tokens and paste the contract address; the symbol and decimals fill in by themselves.

    NextHolders can deposit #SECT in the holder-rewards contract and earn rewards every second, with no lock-up.

07 · Questions

Common questions.

Why do I need ETH if I pay with USDT?

Because Ethereum charges every transaction's network fee in ETH. USDT pays for the #SECT; ETH pays for the transaction that moves it.

Can I sell whenever I want?

Yes. #SECT trades on Uniswap 24 hours a day. Selling is the same swap in reverse, from your own wallet and with no token tax; you only pay the fees Uniswap shows in the quote and the network fee in ETH.

Uniswap shows a warning about #SECT. Is that normal?

Yes. Uniswap flags tokens that aren't on its default lists. Confirm that the address is exactly 0x8C9984B06281f1CA9416e493c2E602AaB08513db and continue.

My wallet app can't calculate the network fee.

Open app.uniswap.org from a computer or from the wallet's own built-in browser. The pool and the route are the same.

The swap failed. What happened?

Usually the price moved while you were confirming, or there wasn't enough ETH for the fee. Your tokens stay in your wallet, although a failed transaction still costs its network fee. Check your ETH, raise the slippage a little and try again.

Can I buy with a bank card?

Not #SECT directly. Buy ETH with your card inside MetaMask or on an exchange, then follow the steps above.
