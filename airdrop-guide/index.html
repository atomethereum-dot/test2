Airdrop Genesis · Step-by-step guide

How to join the Sectora airdrop, step by step.

This guide takes you from your very first click to a Hash Node that is on and counting. Every step says exactly what to press, what you should see and what not to do, with a screenshot. Read one step, do it, then go to the next. About 20 minutes.

Download the PDF guide ↓ Start the guide ↓

The screenshots show an example wallet (0x4b2f…4b3c) and example amounts. Your screen shows your own.

The whole airdrop in one sentence

You need only two things, with the same wallet:

  • 1
    #SECT in stakingAt least 100 #SECT in the official staking, on Ethereum. Part A.
  • 2
    Your Hash Node turned onOn the Sectora test network. 100% free. Part B.

When both are true, your airdrop count starts by itself. No sign-up, no forms.

Already staking #SECT? You're already in.

You don't have to do anything else with your staking. Don't withdraw anything. Skip Part A and go straight to Part B: just turn on your Hash Node with the same wallet you stake with, and you start taking part.

00 · Before you start

Have these four things ready.

01A crypto walletMetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, OKX, Binance Wallet or any Ethereum wallet. On your phone or your computer.
02At least 100 #SECTIn that wallet. Don't have them yet? Buy them on Uniswap with our step-by-step buying guide.
03A little ETHOn Ethereum, in the same wallet. It pays the network fee of the staking (a few dollars).
04 · Most importantONE wallet for everythingYour staking and your node must use the same wallet address. Using two different wallets is the most common mistake.

Three safety rules. Never break them.

1. Never give anyone your secret recovery phrase (the 12 or 24 words) or your private key. Sectora will never ask for them. 2. Only use sectoraorg.com: check the address bar every time. 3. Nobody from Sectora will message you first to “help” you. If someone does, it is a scam.

A

Part A · Steps 1–7 · ≈ 5 minutes

Stake your #SECT

You put your #SECT in the official staking. They stay yours, earn staking rewards every second and count for the airdrop. Already staking? Skip to Part B.

Ethereum mainnet · real #SECTNeeds a little ETH for feesNo lock-up
  1. 01Part A

    Browser

    Open the staking page

    1. Open your browser: Chrome, Safari, or the browser inside your wallet app.On a phone, the easiest is the browser inside your wallet app (in MetaMask: the “Browser” tab).
    2. Type exactly sectoraorg.com/staking and open it.
    3. Scroll down until you see the white box called “Staking console”.
    sectoraorg.com/staking
    Check the address bar. The only real page is sectoraorg.com/staking. If it says anything else, close it.
  2. 02Part A

    Staking console

    Press “Connect wallet”

    1. At the bottom of the Staking console there is a big blue button.
    2. Press Connect wallet.
  3. 03Part A

    Your wallet

    Choose your wallet

    1. A list called “Connect a wallet” opens under the button.
    2. On a computer: press your wallet (for example MetaMask). A window of your wallet opens: press Connect.Wallet on your phone instead? Press “Wallet on your phone” and scan the QR code with your wallet app.
    3. On a phone (Chrome or Safari): tap your wallet app. The app opens: approve the connection, then go back to the browser tab.Inside your wallet app's own browser, it connects directly.
  4. 04Part A

    Check

    Check that you are connected

    1. Look at the big blue button: it now shows your wallet address, short, like 0x4b2f…4b3c.
    2. Under it you now see three buttons: Deposit #SECT Withdraw Withdraw all.
    It worked if you see your address on the button.
    Red message “Switch your wallet to the Ethereum network”? Open your wallet, change the network to Ethereum (Ethereum Mainnet) and reload the page.
  5. 05Part A

    Amount

    Type how many #SECT you stake

    1. Press the box “You deposit”.
    2. Type the number of #SECT. Only the number, for example 1000. No dots, no commas.The console shows what it earns per second, day, month and year before you sign anything.

    How much? At least 100 #SECT to take part in the airdrop. The more you stake, the higher your level:

    Scout100 #SECTprize 55 SECT
    Sentinel500 #SECTprize 325 SECT
    Guardian1,000 #SECTprize 750 SECT
    Titan4,000 #SECTprize 3,600 SECT · no maximum
  6. 06Part A

    Wallet · confirmation 1 of 2

    Press “Deposit #SECT” and approve

    1. Press Deposit #SECT.
    2. Your wallet opens and asks you to approve (a “spending cap”). This only allows the staking contract to take exactly the amount you typed, never more. Nothing moves yet.
    3. Check the details below, then press Confirm in your wallet.
    4. Wait. Don't close the page. It takes from a few seconds to a minute.

    Check this in your wallet before you press Confirm

    Request
    Approve · spending cap
    Amount
    The exact #SECT you typed
    Contract
    0x79Cb8B3B3e81a2B25C6d7250c3C5a742aa8320E0
    Fee
    A small amount of ETH (your wallet shows it)
  7. 07Part A

    Wallet · confirmation 2 of 2

    Confirm the deposit

    1. When the approval is done, your wallet opens a second time by itself, now for the deposit.
    2. Press Confirm again. Wait a few seconds.
    3. A green box appears: “Your rewards · accruing every second”, with “Deposited” and your amount.
    Leave your #SECT in the staking. Only staked #SECT count for the airdrop. Keep them there until you complete your time. You still earn your normal staking rewards.
    Part A done.

    Your #SECT are staked. Now turn on your node with this same wallet.

B

Part B · Steps 8–14 · ≈ 10 minutes

Turn on your Hash Node

The Hash Node runs on Sectora's test network (Sepolia). Everything in Part B is free: you use free test coins that have no value. You never spend real money here.

Free · test networkSame wallet as your stakingStays on by itself
  1. 08Part B

    Dashboard

    Open the dashboard and press “Connect wallet”

    1. Open sectoraorg.com/dashboard.On a phone, open it inside your wallet app's browser (in MetaMask: the “Browser” tab).
    2. At the top right, press Connect wallet.
    Use the SAME wallet you staked with. If your node is on another wallet, it does not count for your airdrop.
  2. 09Part B

    Your wallet

    Choose your wallet and accept the test network

    1. In the list “Connect a wallet”, press your wallet (it says DETECTED).
    2. In your wallet, press Connect.
    3. The dashboard moves your wallet to the Sepolia test network by itself. If your wallet asks to switch or add the network, press Approve / Switch network.
    Your wallet doesn't show Sepolia? Open your wallet settings and turn on “Show test networks”. Then try again.
  3. 10Part B

    Free gas

    Get free Sepolia ETH for gas

    1. Every action on the test network needs a little Sepolia ETH. It is a free test coin with no value.
    2. Go down to “Faucet & rewards”. In the box “Testnet faucet”, press Get Sepolia ETH ↗.
    3. A Google Cloud page opens. Paste your wallet address and press the button to receive the free Sepolia ETH.To copy your address: open your wallet and tap your account name or address. If the page asks, sign in with any Google account.
    4. Come back to the dashboard tab.
    What you receive is enough for every step of Part B.
  4. 11Part B

    Free test tokens

    Claim 5,000 free tSECT

    1. In the same box, press Claim 5,000 tSECT.
    2. Your wallet opens: press Confirm.
    3. Wait a few seconds. “Your balance” now shows 5,000 tSECT.
    tSECT are test tokens with no money value. You can claim them again every 24 hours.
  5. 12Part B

    Hash market

    Buy the Node Kit · 50 TH/s

    1. Go down to “Hash market”.
    2. Open the tab Physical node (1).
    3. Press the card 50 TH/s · 4,300 tSECT, marked AIRDROP PLAN (2). This is the node that works for the airdrop.
    4. Press Buy 50 TH/s (3).
    5. Your wallet opens two times: first to allow the payment, then to confirm the purchase. Press Confirm both times.
    The 4,300 tSECT come from the 5,000 free tSECT of step 11. No real money.
  6. 13Part B

    Your node

    Register your node. The location is automatic.

    1. Go down to “Your node”.
    2. Your node's location is already detected (1) and its name is already filled in. You don't type anything.
    3. Press Register node (2).
    4. Your wallet opens: press Confirm.
    About the location. Our systems detect your node's approximate location from your connection and fill it in for you. Only the city is saved on the network, never your address. The location is wrong (for example because of a VPN)? Press “Change” and correct it.

    Step 13 · Plan B · only if you see “We couldn't detect the location”: enter it by hand

    Why
    Sometimes a VPN, an ad-blocker or a strict browser blocks the detection. Then two boxes appear: Latitude and Longitude.
    Find the numbers
    Open Google Maps and search for your city. On a computer, right-click the city center; on a phone, press and hold it. You see two numbers, like 40.4168, -3.7038.
    Fill them in
    The first number goes in Latitude (1). The second number goes in Longitude (2). Keep the minus sign if there is one.
    Then
    Press Register node and confirm in your wallet. Use your city, not your home.
  7. 14Part B

    Done

    Done: your node is on

    1. The button changes to Registered.
    2. Your node appears on the network map. From now on, it counts.
    YESYou can close the tabYour node stays on in the network by itself.
    YESYou can turn off your computerYour node does not depend on your computer or your phone.
    NONever deactivate your nodeA deactivated node stops counting until you turn it on again.
    Part B done. You are in the airdrop.

    Staking + node on, with the same wallet. Your count is running.

Problems · quick answers

Something didn't work? Look here first.

“Not enough ETH” on the staking page

Part A uses real Ethereum. Add a little ETH (on Ethereum) to the same wallet and try again.

“Not enough gas” on the dashboard

Part B uses free Sepolia ETH. Repeat step 10.

My wallet doesn't show Sepolia

In your wallet settings, turn on “Show test networks”. Then reload the dashboard.

“Switch your wallet to the Ethereum network”

You are on the staking page with the wrong network. In your wallet, choose Ethereum (Ethereum Mainnet) and reload.

“You do not have that many #SECT in your wallet”

Type a smaller amount, or buy more #SECT with our buying guide.

I don't have enough tSECT to buy the Node Kit

Claim 5,000 tSECT in step 11. Already claimed today? Wait 24 hours and claim again.

I used a different wallet for the node

It doesn't count. Connect your staking wallet on the dashboard and repeat Part B with it.

The location wasn't detected, or it is wrong

Press “Change”, or follow the box “enter it by hand” in step 13. Use your city, not your home.

The wallet window didn't open

Open your wallet app or extension yourself: the request is waiting there. On a phone, after approving, go back to the browser tab.

Do I have to keep my computer on?

No. Once registered, your node stays on by itself. Just never deactivate it.

Do I have to withdraw my staking to join?

No. Never. Your staked #SECT count as they are, and you keep earning your staking rewards.

Does Part B cost money?

No. tSECT and Sepolia ETH are free test coins with no value.
