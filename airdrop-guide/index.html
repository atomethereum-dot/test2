00 · Before you start

Have these four things ready.

01A crypto walletMetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, OKX, Binance Wallet or any Ethereum wallet. On your phone or your computer.
02At least 100 #SECTIn that wallet. Don't have them yet? Buy them on Uniswap with our step-by-step buying guide.
03A little ETHOn Ethereum, in the same wallet. It pays the network fees (a few dollars).
04 · Most importantONE wallet for everythingYour staking and your node must use the same wallet address. Using two different wallets is the most common mistake.

Three safety rules. Never break them.

1. Never give anyone your secret recovery phrase (the 12 or 24 words) or your private key. Sectora will never ask for them. 2. Only use sectoraorg.com: check the address bar every time. 3. Nobody from Sectora will message you first to “help” you. If someone does, it is a scam.

Computer or phone

Computer or phone? Both work.

Use the one you have. The steps are the same: only the way your wallet opens changes.

On a computer

Using a computer? Do it like this.

On a computer your wallet is a browser extension (Chrome, Brave or Edge). It opens as a small window at the top right.

  1. Install your wallet in Chrome, Brave or Edge (for example MetaMask, only from its official website). Open it and unlock it with your password.
  2. Staking (Part A): open sectoraorg.com/staking and press Connect wallet. Press your wallet in the list (1). The wallet window opens at the top right: press Connect (2).
  3. Node (Part B): open sectoraorg.com/dashboard in the same browser, press Connect wallet and choose the same wallet.

Don't see the wallet window? Click the puzzle icon at the top right of the browser, then your wallet. No extension? In the list, choose Wallet on your phone and scan the QR code with your phone.

On a phone

Using a phone? Read this first.

Everything in this guide works on a phone. You only need your wallet app installed (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet…).

  1. Staking (Part A): open sectoraorg.com/staking in Chrome or Safari and press Connect wallet. Tap your wallet app in the list, approve there and come back to the browser.
  2. Node (Part B): open sectoraorg.com/dashboard and press Connect wallet. On a phone it shows Open in MetaMask (or your wallet): tap it and the dashboard opens inside your wallet app. Continue there.
  3. Inside the wallet app everything connects directly. Use the same wallet app for everything.

If the wallet app doesn't open, open it yourself, find its built-in browser (often called Browser, Explore or Discover) and type the address there.

Part B · Steps 8–14

Turn on your Hash Node

The Hash Node runs on Sectora's test network (Sepolia). Everything in Part B is free: you use free test coins that have no value. You never spend real money here.

Free · test networkSame wallet as your stakingStays on by itself
  1. 08Part B

    Dashboard

    Open the dashboard and press “Connect wallet”

    1. Open sectoraorg.com/dashboard.
    2. At the top right, press Connect wallet.
    3. On a phone (Chrome or Safari): a list appears. Tap Open in MetaMask (or your wallet) and the dashboard opens inside your wallet app. Press Connect wallet again there.
    Use the SAME wallet you staked with. If your node is on another wallet, it does not count for your airdrop.
  2. 09Part B

    Your wallet

    Choose your wallet and accept the test network

    1. In the list “Connect a wallet”, press your wallet (it says Detected).
    2. In your wallet, press Connect.
    3. The dashboard moves your wallet to the Sepolia test network by itself. If your wallet asks to switch or add the network, press Approve or Switch network.
    Your wallet doesn't show Sepolia? Open your wallet settings and turn on “Show test networks”. Then try again.
  3. 10Part B

    Free gas

    Get free Sepolia ETH for gas

    1. Every action on the test network needs a little Sepolia ETH. It is a free test coin with no value.
    2. In the dashboard, go down a little to “How it works”. In box 02 “Get free gas”, press Get Sepolia ETH.
    3. A Google Cloud page opens. Check that the network is Ethereum Sepolia, paste your wallet address in “Wallet address or ENS name” and press Get 0.05 Sepolia ETH. If it asks, sign in with a Google account.
    4. Come back to the dashboard tab.
    How do I copy my address? Open your wallet and tap your account name or address at the top: it copies by itself.
    Google says you need 0.001 ETH on Ethereum Mainnet? Google's faucet only gives Sepolia ETH to wallets that hold at least 0.001 real ETH on Ethereum. Keep a little ETH in your wallet (you need it for Part A anyway) and try again.
  4. 11Part B

    Free test tokens

    Claim 5,000 free tSECT

    1. In the same box, press Claim 5,000 tSECT.
    2. Your wallet opens: press Confirm.
    3. Wait a few seconds. “Your balance” now shows 5,000 tSECT.
    tSECT are test tokens with no money value. You can claim them again every 24 hours.
  5. 12Part B

    Hash market

    Buy the Node Kit · 50 TH/s

    1. Go down to “Hash market”.
    2. Open the tab Physical node (1).
    3. Press the card 50 TH/s · 4,300 tSECT, marked Airdrop plan (2). This is the node that works for the airdrop.
    4. Press Buy 50 TH/s (3).
    5. Your wallet opens two times: first to allow the payment, then to confirm the purchase. Press Confirm both times.
    The 4,300 tSECT come from the 5,000 free tSECT of step 11. No real money.
  6. 13Part B

    Your node

    Register your node. The location is automatic.

    1. Go down to “Your node”.
    2. Your node's location is already detected (1) and its name is already filled in. You don't type anything.
    3. Press Register node (2).
    4. Your wallet opens: press Confirm.
    About the location. Our systems detect your node's approximate location from your connection and fill it in for you. Only the city is saved on the network, never your address. The location is wrong (for example because of a VPN)? Press “Change” and correct it.

    Step 13 · Plan B · only if you see “We couldn't detect the location. Enter it below.”

    Why
    Sometimes a VPN, an ad-blocker or a strict browser blocks the detection. Then two boxes appear: Latitude and Longitude.
    Find the numbers
    Open Google Maps and search for your city. On a computer, right-click the city center; on a phone, press and hold it. You see two numbers, like 40.4168, -3.7038.
    Fill them in
    The first number goes in Latitude (1). The second number goes in Longitude (2). Keep the minus sign if there is one.
    Then
    Press Register node and confirm in your wallet. Use your city, not your home.

Problems · quick answers

Something didn't work? Look here first.

“Not enough ETH” on the staking page

Part A uses real Ethereum. Add a little ETH (on Ethereum) to the same wallet and try again.

“Not enough gas” on the dashboard

Part B uses free Sepolia ETH. Repeat step 10.

Google's faucet asks for 0.001 ETH on Ethereum

Your wallet needs at least 0.001 real ETH on Ethereum to use Google's faucet. Keep a little ETH in it and try again, or write to support.

My wallet doesn't show Sepolia

In your wallet settings, turn on “Show test networks”. Then reload the dashboard.

“Switch your wallet to the Ethereum network”

You are on the staking page with the wrong network. In your wallet, choose Ethereum (Ethereum Mainnet) and reload.

“You do not have that many #SECT in your wallet”

Type a smaller amount, or buy more #SECT with our buying guide.

I don't have enough tSECT to buy the Node Kit

Claim 5,000 tSECT in step 11. Already claimed today? Wait 24 hours and claim again.

I used a different wallet for the node

It doesn't count. Connect your staking wallet on the dashboard and repeat Part B with it.

The location wasn't detected, or it is wrong

Press “Change”, or follow Plan B in step 13. Use your city, not your home.

The wallet window didn't open

Open your wallet app or extension yourself: the request is waiting there. On a phone, after approving, go back to the browser tab.

Do I have to keep my computer on?

No. Once registered, your node stays on by itself. Just never deactivate it.

Do I have to withdraw my staking to join?

No. Never. Your staked #SECT count as they are, and you keep earning your staking rewards.

Does Part B cost money?

No. tSECT and Sepolia ETH are free test coins with no value.
