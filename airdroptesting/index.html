    09 · FAQ

    Simple answers.

    Still unsure? Ask in the Telegram. The team answers there.

    01Is it really free?

    Yes. The Scout level costs nothing and needs no gas: you only sign a message. Holding SECT is optional and unlocks more points.

    02Do I have to send my SECT anywhere?

    No. Your SECT stays in your wallet. Once a day we read your public balance. If anyone asks you to send tokens for this airdrop, it is a scam.

    03How much will I get?

    It depends on your points compared with everyone else's at the end of the campaign. Top 100 prizes are fixed by rank. We don't promise amounts in dollars.

    04When can I claim?

    When SectoraEVM launches: 50% then, and 50% 90 days later. If the launch passes the guaranteed deadline, the claim opens on Ethereum.

    05What is a valid referral?

    A friend who joins from your link with a new wallet, reaches the Sentinel minimum and passes the anti-bot check. Fake wallets are removed.

    06What if I sell below the minimum?

    You keep the points you already earned. Your streak starts again and holding points stop until you are back above the minimum.

    07Who can take part?

    Anyone where it is legal. The final rules, including any excluded countries, will be published after legal review and before Season 1.
