08 · Questions

Simple answers.

Still unsure? Ask in our Telegram.

01Is it free?

Testing the node is free and registers you for phase 2 benefits. To win SECT in phase 1 you need at least 100 SECT in staking: as Scout you win 40 SECT.

02Why do I need to stake SECT to win more?

Because SECT already trades. The famous airdrops gave their token away on the day it was born, when there was no market to protect yet. With a token that is already on the market, giving it to people with nothing at stake usually ends in sales the same day, and that hurts the holders who already back Sectora.

Asking you to stake SECT protects the community in four ways:
1. It rewards the people who really support the project.
2. It stops people who move the same SECT between many wallets to win several times.
3. The SECT stay in staking, off the market, and you keep earning the staking rewards.
4. The prizes arrive on SectoraEVM in two halves, instead of hitting the market at once.

Testing the Hash Node is still free and registers you for phase 2.

03What is a node? Does it cost anything?

A node is your place in the Sectora network. For now it runs on the testnet: the test SECT are free and have no value, and you only need a little free test ETH for gas (the dashboard links to it). You don't need a computer switched on. Use the same wallet as in the airdrop so your node counts.

04Do I have to send my SECT?

Only to Sectora staking, which has no lock-up: you withdraw whenever you want. You never send SECT to any other address. We check your staking once a day.

05Are my SECT locked? Can I sell?

No. Staking has no lock-up: you withdraw and sell whenever you want, and you keep your staking rewards. Only the airdrop changes: if you leave before day 30 you win nothing; from day 30 you keep what you've earned (with 45 days, half the prize). What isn't won goes back to the bag. If you come back later, your count starts again from that day.

06How much will I get?

It depends on your level: Scout 40 SECT (100 in staking), Sentinel 250 SECT (500), Guardian 600 SECT (1,000) and Titan 3,400 SECT per spot, where each 4,000 SECT in staking is one spot, with no maximum. You win the full prize when you complete 90 days. Spots stay open until the 7,000,000 SECT are reserved. We don't promise amounts in dollars.

07When do I get it?

On SectoraEVM. If you complete your 90 days before mainnet, you receive it at mainnet launch; if you complete them later, when your 90 days end. Half that day and the other half 90 days later.

08I already stake. Do I have to do anything?

Almost nothing: you're already in. Your staked SECT count on their own and you don't need to withdraw anything. You only need to connect your node with the same wallet and you're done. Staking has no lock-up.

09Who can join?

Anyone where it's legal. The final rules will be published before the airdrop opens.

10What is Airdrop 2?

A future airdrop for the people who use their Genesis SECT on SectoraEVM. Its rules will be published before it starts.

11How do you stop one person from using many wallets?

With the math of the levels. The percentage grows with each level: Scout 40%, Sentinel 50%, Guardian 60% and Titan 85%. So splitting your SECT across wallets always pays less: 4,000 SECT as one Titan win 3,400 SECT; split into 40 Scouts they win 1,600. On top of that, before paying we check the blockchain and remove groups of wallets that come from the same person.

12I joined late. Do I win less?

No. Everyone has their own 90 days, counted from the day they join. If you were already staking, your 90 days start on the opening day. If you add more SECT later, the new spot counts its own 90 days from the day you add it. Entries stay open until the 7,000,000 SECT are reserved.

13Will there be a phase 2?

Yes. Phase 2 starts when phase 1 ends. It hasn't been revealed yet: its tasks will be announced on X and Telegram. Phase 1 participants will get extra benefits. Phase 1 doesn't change: 90 days in staking with your node on wins your level's full prize.
