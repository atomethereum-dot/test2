08 · Questions

Simple answers.

Still unsure? Ask in our Telegram.

01Is it free?

Yes. You connect your wallet and test the node on the testnet, which is free. Holding SECT is optional and makes you win more.

02What is a node? Does it cost anything?

A node is your place in the Sectora network. For now it runs on the testnet: the test SECT are free and have no value, and you only need a little free test ETH for gas (the dashboard links to it). You don't need a computer switched on. Use the same wallet as in the airdrop so your node counts.

03Do I have to send my SECT?

No. Your SECT stay in your wallet. We only look at your balance once a day.

04Are my SECT locked? Can I sell?

They are never locked and you never send them anywhere. You can sell whenever you want. If you sell before mainnet launches, you move down to the level that matches what you have left.

05How much will I get?

It depends on your level: Scout 300 SECT, Sentinel 375 SECT, Guardian 800 SECT and Titan 3,000 SECT. One prize per person, for the first ones in each level. We don't promise amounts in dollars.

06When do I get it?

When SectoraEVM mainnet launches, directly on SectoraEVM: half that day and the other half 90 days later.

07I already stake. Do I have to do anything?

Almost nothing: you're already in. Your staked SECT count on their own and you don't need to withdraw anything. You only need to connect your node with the same wallet and you're done. Staking has no lock-up.

08Who can join?

Anyone where it's legal. The final rules will be published before the airdrop opens.

09What is Airdrop 2?

A future airdrop for the people who use their Genesis SECT on SectoraEVM. Its rules will be published before it starts.
