Why staking instead of an airdrop

A growth program paid by the treasury, for a limited time

Most projects grow by giving tokens away. Those tokens land with people who have no reason to keep them, and they are sold into the market the same week. The Foundation chose the opposite: put the same treasury budget behind the people who hold.

Airdrop Tokens given away

New supply goes to wallets that did nothing to earn it. Most of it is sold, and every holder pays for that in price.

Staking season Rewards for holding

Rewards go only to #SECT that stays staked, and half of them are paid at the close. Holding is what pays.

An airdrop pays people to leave. This season pays people to stay. It is safer for the project and it scales with the people who believe in it.

The 24-hour window

Nothing is locked. Leaving just has a clock on it.
01 You withdraw

Your #SECT comes back immediately

You can take out part or all of your stake at any moment, with no penalty and no waiting period. The moment your balance drops below its highest point, a 24-hour window opens.

02 Within 24 hours

Put it back and nothing is lost

Deposit the amount again before the window closes and everything you had accrued is still there, monthly and final balance alike. The season carries on as if you never left.

03 After 24 hours

Uncollected rewards go back to the pool

If the window closes, every reward you had not collected yet is forfeited and returns to the reward pool for the stakers who stayed. Anything you already collected is yours to keep.

04 No shortcuts

Leaving one token behind does not count

The window opens whenever your balance falls below its peak, not only when it reaches zero. Withdrawing almost everything and leaving a dust amount inside does not keep the rewards.

Why this isn't just a promised number

The rules live in the contract, not in an announcement
01 Funded before it's paid

Every reward is deposited first

#SECT has no mint function and its ownership is renounced. The treasury transfers the reward budget into the contract before it is paid out, and the size of that pool is public on-chain.

02 No pulling out

The treasury is committed for the season

Once #SECT is in the reward pool, the contract does not let the owner take it back until the twelve months are over. Rewards already earned by stakers are out of the owner's reach entirely.

03 Fixed in code

Nobody can change the rules mid-season

7% a month, the 3.5% / 3.5% split, the twelve-month season and the 24-hour window are constants in the contract. They cannot be edited after anyone has staked.

04 Your deposit is yours

Principal never pays anyone else

Staked #SECT and reward #SECT are accounted separately. No function lets a deposit be paid out as someone else's reward, and withdrawing always works, even if the pool is empty.

Frequently asked

What to know before staking #SECT
Can I stake right now?

Not yet. The staking contract is written and covered by an automated test suite, but it has not been deployed to Ethereum mainnet, so there is no address to stake into today. When it goes live this page switches over on its own.

Yes. Connect your wallet above, enter an amount and stake. Rewards start accruing from that moment.

When can I collect my rewards?

Every reward is split in two as it accrues. The monthly half (3.5% a month) can be collected one month after your first deposit, and then once a month. The other half (3.5% a month) builds up and is paid in one go when the twelve-month season closes, together with any monthly balance you have not collected.

Can I unstake whenever I want?

Yes. There is no lockup and no penalty on your deposit. If you withdraw, you have 24 hours to put it back and keep everything you had accrued. After that, rewards you had not collected yet go back to the pool. While the window is open the monthly collection waits until your stake is restored.

Why a staking season and not an airdrop?

An airdrop creates new sellers: people who received tokens for free and sell them. This program spends the treasury budget only on #SECT that stays staked, and pays half of it at the end. It is a growth program designed to reward holders without pushing the price down, with a hard cap so its cost to the treasury is known in advance.

Where does the reward money come from?

From the Sectora Foundation treasury, deposited into the contract in advance. No new tokens are created. If the reward pool ever ran out, rewards would pause until it is refilled, instead of promising more than the contract holds.

Is there a limit on how much can be staked?

Yes. The whole program takes at most 10,000,000 #SECT, a limit written into the contract. Once it is full, new deposits are refused until space frees up. If you withdraw and the program fills up in the meantime, you can still put your own amount back within your 24-hour window.

Can I join after the season has started?

Yes, as long as the season is open. The season has one fixed closing date for everyone, so you earn 7% a month for the months you are in, and your final balance is paid at that closing date.
