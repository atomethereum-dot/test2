Why this isn't just a promised number

The rules live in the contract
01 Funded before it's paid

Every reward is deposited first

#SECT has no mint function and its ownership is renounced. The treasury transfers the reward budget into the contract before it is paid out, and the size of that pool is public on-chain.

02 Fixed in code

The rate cannot change

14.9% APY and the 10,000,000 #SECT cap are constants in the contract. Nobody can edit them after deployment.

03 Your deposit is yours

Deposits never pay anyone else

Deposits and rewards are accounted separately. No function lets the owner touch a deposit or a reward already earned, and withdrawing always works.

Frequently asked

What to know before depositing #SECT
Can I deposit right now?

Not yet. The contract is written and covered by an automated test suite, but it has not been deployed to Ethereum mainnet. When it goes live this page switches over on its own.

When can I collect my rewards?

Whenever you want. Rewards accrue every second and you can claim them at any time, including after you have withdrawn your deposit.

Can I withdraw whenever I want?

Yes. There is no lock-up and no penalty. You can take out part or all of your deposit at any moment, and everything you earned until then stays claimable.

Where does the reward money come from?

From the Sectora Foundation treasury, deposited into the contract in advance. No new tokens are created. If the reward pool ever ran out, rewards would pause until it is refilled.

Is there a limit on how much can be deposited?

Yes. The program holds at most 10,000,000 #SECT, a limit written into the contract. Once it is full, new deposits are refused until someone withdraws.
