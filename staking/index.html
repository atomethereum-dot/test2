Rewards are paid from #SECT the treasury deposits into the contract in advance; nothing is minted. The foundation can top up the reward pool, take back the part not yet earned and adjust the rate, which only counts from that second on. Deposits and rewards already earned stay yours. If the pool ran dry, rewards would stop accruing until it is refilled. The contract has been tested but not externally audited.

03Security

Security

Why this isn't just a promised number.

Four rules written into the contract. Each one can be checked on Etherscan without asking the foundation for anything.

04 rules

01

Every reward is deposited first

#SECT has no mint function and its ownership is renounced. The treasury transfers the reward budget into the contract before it is paid out, and the size of that pool is public on-chain.

Read the pool on Etherscan ↗
02

What you earn stays earned

The APY starts at 14.9% and the foundation can adjust it on-chain; a change only counts from that second on, so nothing already earned is ever recalculated.

Read the code ↗
03

Deposits never pay anyone else

Deposits and rewards are accounted separately. No function lets the owner touch a deposit or a reward already earned, and withdrawing always works.

Read the code ↗
04

You approve only what you deposit

This page asks your wallet to approve the exact amount you are depositing, never an unlimited allowance. The deposit uses all of it, so nothing stays approved afterwards.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

What to know before depositing #SECT.

06 questions

01Can I deposit right now?

Yes. Connect your wallet in the console above, enter an amount and deposit. Rewards start accruing from that moment.

02When can I collect my rewards?

Whenever you want. Rewards accrue every second and you can claim them at any time, including after you have withdrawn your deposit.

03Can I withdraw whenever I want?

Yes. There is no lock-up and no penalty. You can take out part or all of your deposit at any moment, and everything you earned until then stays claimable.

04Where does the reward money come from?

From the Sectora Foundation treasury, deposited into the contract in advance. No new tokens are created. If the reward pool ever ran out, rewards would pause until it is refilled.

05Is there a limit on how much can be deposited?

No. There is no cap on deposits. The foundation can close the program to new deposits; anyone already in can still withdraw and claim at any time.

06How do I know I'm on the real contract?

When you approve, your wallet shows the address that receives the approval. It must be 0x79Cb8B3B3e81a2B25C6d7250c3C5a742aa8320E0, the same one shown in the console and on Etherscan. If it is different, stop.
