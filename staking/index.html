Why this isn't just a promised number

The mechanism behind the 14.9% target, in three parts
01 Revenue, not emissions

Funded by hash market sales

Every hash purchase splits by a fixed, published rule: 80% buys back and burns #SECT on the open market, 20% funds APY rewards, protocol expansion and the team. Nothing is minted to pay stakers.

02 Deflationary by design

The other 80% shrinks supply

Every #SECT bought back with hash revenue is burned instantly — permanently removed from circulating supply. Staking rewards and supply reduction are funded by the same transaction, not competing budgets.

03 Verify it yourself

Read from the chain

Buybacks, burns and reward payouts are on-chain events — check them in the Dashboard's Hash Market panel or directly on the explorer, instead of taking our word for it.

Frequently asked

What to know before staking #SECT
Is the 14.9% APY active right now?

Staking is open and your #SECT starts accruing from the moment hash market revenue activates — the 14.9% is the target rate that revenue is projected to support, published so you can hold it accountable, not a rate we're paying out of thin air before there's real revenue behind it.

Can I unstake whenever I want?

Yes. This is flexible staking — no lockup period, no early-unstake penalty. Your principal is available on demand; only accrued rewards depend on revenue timing.

Where does the reward money actually come from?

20% of every hash market sale, published and verifiable on-chain. It is not paid from new token issuance, presale proceeds or the treasury — if hash market revenue is zero, realized rewards are zero, regardless of what the target rate says.

How is this different from staking on the Dashboard?

Same mechanism, same contract — this page is the dedicated place to understand the mechanics and estimate rewards. Wallet connection and live position management happen in the Dashboard.
