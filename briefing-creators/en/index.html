Note on project revenue

Revenue generated by these products is split under a fixed rule enforced by the protocol itself. On hash power sales and DEX fees, 80% goes to buying back $SECT on the market and 20% to APY rewards, protocol expansion and the team. On L3 network transaction fees, 67% goes to buybacks and 33% to validator rewards, expansion and the team. Bought-back $SECT is permanently removed from the supply. This revenue will begin once the products operate on mainnet.

04How to buy (to explain on screen)

  1. Install an Ethereum-compatible wallet, such as MetaMask or Trust Wallet.
  2. Hold USDT on the Ethereum network and a small amount of ETH for the network fee.
  3. Buy $SECT only on the official Uniswap website (app.uniswap.org), using this official link:Open the official Uniswap link ↗
  4. Check that the contract address is 0x8C9984B06281f1CA9416e493c2E602AaB08513db.
  5. Connect the wallet, enter the USDT amount, review and confirm the transaction.
  6. If the token does not appear in the wallet, import it using the contract address.
