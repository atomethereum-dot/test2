    01What Sectora is

    What Sectora is

    Security software for the crypto ecosystem and the real assets moving on-chain.

    Sectora builds the software that secures blockchain systems and the real-world assets tokenized on them: contracts audited before they are trusted with value, custody verified against the asset standing behind the token, and monitoring that keeps running long after launch.

    Sectora Foundation is a company registered in Illinois, United States. #SECT is the network token: contract renounced, no mint function, and every record open to anyone who wants to read it.

    02Founders' statement

    Founders' statement

    Ref. SF·ST·01·26

    #SECT is not a pump‑and‑dump token.

    It is a token built to be held as the project progresses and scales, not for short-term speculation.

    Sectora’s founders are clear: the fixed supply, the renounced contract, the locked liquidity and the public audit remove the mechanisms that pump‑and‑dump schemes rely on.

    Every holder is free to buy and sell whenever they choose, without restrictions.

    The project advances with each milestone, starting today: from the products already live on testnet to the 50% supply reduction in March 2027.

    04Distribution

    Distribution

    No presale. No rounds. Only the open market.

    Sectora has never sold #SECT ahead of its listing. There was no presale, no private round, no seed allocation and no venture financing, so no one is holding tokens bought at a price the public was never offered.

    The token lives entirely on the open market. Liquidity is live on Uniswap, where anyone can buy or sell #SECT at any moment, at the price the market sets — with no allowlist, no minimum, no lock-up and no permission from the foundation.
