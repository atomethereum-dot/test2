Frequently asked

What to know before joining Round 1
When do I receive my #SECT tokens?

25% unlocks at TGE (Token Generation Event, at listing). The remaining 75% vests linearly over 60 days to keep early sell pressure controlled after listing.

What happens if the round doesn't fill?

If the $2,000,000 cap isn't reached by the closing date, the round still finalizes at whatever amount was raised — presale terms don't require hitting the cap to proceed. Refund conditions, if any, are published in the final terms at close.

Can I participate from any country?

No. Participation is restricted in certain jurisdictions, including the United States for non-accredited participants, pending final terms. Check the legal disclosure before connecting a wallet.

Is this the same contract that lists on Uniswap?

Yes — #SECT is a single ERC-20 contract. The presale allocates supply ahead of the public listing at a fixed discount; it does not create a separate token.
